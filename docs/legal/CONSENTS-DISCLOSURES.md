# Consents and Disclosures

## Required disclosure families
- Terms of Use acceptance
- Privacy Policy acknowledgement
- Marketing communication preferences
- Transactional communication notice
- Provider agreements and attestations
- Verified Professional limitations
- AI-assisted content notice
- Delivery/third-party processor notice
- Location/service-address disclosure where applicable
- Upload rights/authorization confirmation
- Sensitive-data warning for support and AI fields
- Refund/cancellation terms at point of booking/checkout

Consent must be affirmative when required. A pre-checked box is not treated as evidence of affirmative consent for a sensitive operation. Consent records store policy version, type, result, source and timestamp.
