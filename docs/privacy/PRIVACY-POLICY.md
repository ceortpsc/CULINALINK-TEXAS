# CulinaLinkTX Privacy Policy — Operational Draft

**Policy version:** 2026-09-12

This is an operational privacy-policy baseline and must receive legal review before being treated as the final policy for every jurisdiction.

## Data categories
CulinaLinkTX may process account identifiers and contact information; provider/business profile data; service locations and fulfillment addresses; booking/order/financial metadata; support records; compliance metadata; security/audit events; and user-requested AI prompts or generated creative drafts.

## Purposes
Data is processed to authenticate users, operate the marketplace, fulfill bookings/orders, coordinate pickup/delivery, support providers/customers, maintain compliance/audit records, prevent fraud, secure the service, provide requested AI-assisted features and satisfy applicable legal obligations.

## Sharing/processors
Data may be transmitted to configured processors needed for authentication, delivery, payment/payout, communications, storage or other requested features. CulinaLinkTX does not treat an external processor as the sole authoritative copy of core marketplace lifecycle records.

## Sensitive data
Sensitive data is minimized. Raw passwords, full payment-card credentials and backend signing secrets are not stored in ordinary marketplace records. Users should not enter full SSNs/TINs, card numbers, passwords or bank credentials into ordinary support or AI fields.

## Rights and requests
Where applicable, users may request access, correction, deletion, portability, opt-out review or appeal through the in-app Privacy & Data Protection Center. Requests are authenticated and reviewed. Data may be retained when necessary for tax, accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, security, legal obligations or auditability.

## Retention
Retention is purpose-based. Account, financial, support, compliance and security records have different retention needs. Eligible data is deleted or deidentified after approved retention review.

## Security
CulinaLinkTX uses secure transport, server-side authorization, least privilege, audit controls, secret isolation, bounded workers and incident-response procedures. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## AI
Andreaa and creative/campaign features process user-provided prompts and account-scoped context only for requested functions. AI output is advisory or draft content and cannot independently approve protected lifecycle states.

## Contact
Privacy requests should be submitted through the Privacy & Data Protection Center or Contact Us workflow so the request can be authenticated, tracked and audited.
