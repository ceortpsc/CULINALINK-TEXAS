# Data Protection Standard

## Classification
- PUBLIC: intentionally public marketplace profile/catalog data.
- INTERNAL: operational configuration and non-sensitive internal records.
- PRIVATE: account, booking, support and fulfillment data.
- RESTRICTED: compliance evidence, security/audit data, financial/tax metadata and secrets.

## Controls
1. Data minimization and purpose limitation.
2. Server-side authentication and authorization.
3. Encryption in transit and platform-managed protection at rest where supported.
4. Secret values never returned to browsers or logs.
5. Private addresses hidden unless intentionally designated for public pickup.
6. Upload validation and private storage by default.
7. Retention classes and deletion/deidentification review.
8. Incident detection, containment, recovery and post-incident review.
9. Processor inventory and contract review before production integration.
10. Data-protection assessment for high-risk processing where legally applicable.

## Texas baseline
Texas privacy law may provide rights to access, correct, delete, obtain portable data and opt out of certain targeted advertising, sale or significant-decision profiling, depending on applicability. CulinaLinkTX implements request categories supporting these operations without assuming every statutory provision applies to every user or business scenario.
