# Tests

## Test 1 - Publish a real live culinary gig and retain it [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: production-live mode, POST /api/gig-studio/preflight, POST /api/gig-studio/draft, POST /api/gig-studio/publish, GET /api/gigs, persistence after refresh
Description: Verifies the primary live marketplace write creates a governed real gig record rather than a demo or simulated opportunity.
Steps:
1. Sign in, open Post a Gig, enter the title "QA Live Private Dinner", Killeen, a future date, CLIENT_HOME, six guests, one professional, a valid description and positive budget
2. Run compliance preflight, save the draft, then select Validate & publish
3. Refresh the page and inspect Open opportunities
Expected: The gig is OPEN or held in COMPLIANCE_REVIEW according to its real preflight result; if OPEN, it remains visible after refresh and is never labeled Demo or Sandbox.
