# Tests

## Test 1 - Discover, save, and request a demo culinary professional [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: GET /api/marketplace/live, public realtime subscription boundary, Google/Apple/X authentication entry, POST/GET/DELETE /api/v1/favorites, POST /api/bookings, Discover grid/map/list/swipe views, demo-booking truth state
Description: Verifies the primary marketplace workflow uses real persisted Favorites and keeps demo booking requests explicitly non-production.

Steps:
1. Open Discover, confirm culinary professionals load, switch to Map and List, then return to Grid
2. Sign in, save Chef Jordan to Favorites, open Favorites, and confirm Chef Jordan is visible for the signed-in account
3. Return to Discover, request Chef Jordan as a sandbox booking, open Orders and confirm the booking is represented as a demo/non-production request; then remove Chef Jordan from Favorites and refresh Favorites

Expected: Discovery views work without exposing private realtime entities; the Favorite persists until deleted and remains deleted after refresh; the demo booking appears only as a sandbox/demo request and is never represented as a real confirmed service.

## Test 2 - Build and publish a governed culinary gig
Viewport: desktop
Covers: POST /api/gig-studio/preflight, POST /api/gig-studio/draft, POST /api/gig-studio/publish, GET /api/gigs, compliance preflight, authenticated draft persistence, workflow-task creation
Description: Verifies a valid client-home culinary gig moves through draft validation and becomes an OPEN marketplace opportunity without treating preflight as a government permit.

Steps:
1. Sign in, open Post a Gig, keep venue class CLIENT_HOME, enter a unique title "QA Private Dinner", a description longer than 20 characters, Killeen, a future date, guest count 6, one professional and a positive budget
2. Run compliance preflight and confirm R1_PRIVATE_CHEF_CLIENT_SITE with PRELIMINARY_ELIGIBLE plus the disclaimer that preflight is not a permit or legal determination
3. Save the draft, then Validate & publish and confirm the resulting status is OPEN; refresh and confirm "QA Private Dinner" appears in Open opportunities

Expected: Required-field validation passes, a resumable authenticated draft is created, the client-home preflight remains advisory, publishing creates an OPEN non-demo gig, and the public listing remains visible after refresh.

Description: Verifies mobile provider flows never convert an incomplete profile or uploaded/pending record into a verified professional.

Steps:
1. Open Verified Professionals and confirm the page explains badge meaning, evidence review, validity and explicit limitations including no government endorsement
2. Sign in with an account that has not completed provider onboarding, accept all verification attestations and attempt a Verified Professional application
3. Open Provider Onboarding and confirm the workflow remains IN_PROGRESS or incomplete; then open Discover and confirm demo/pending professionals do not display an active CulinaLinkTX Verified badge

Steps:
1. Open Pickup & Delivery and verify the DoorDash worker shows its actual environment, access-key/webhook readiness, and the warning that sandbox activity does not dispatch a real Dasher
2. While signed out attempt to use a protected delivery/account action and confirm sign-in is required; sign in and confirm My delivery records remain account-scoped
3. Open Rapid Pay and confirm actual card issuance is not shown as enabled without authoritative provider setup/KYC, and that the page distinguishes contractor Disbursements from employee payroll

Expected: External delivery and payout capabilities expose truthful configuration state; private delivery records require authentication and owner-scoped subscriptions; sandbox or missing credentials never produce a real-dispatch claim; and card ISSUED/ACTIVE states are never fabricated.
